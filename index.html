🧘 Meditate & Find Your Calm

Simple, science-backed daily practices designed to bring peace to your mind in just minutes.

🌬️

1. Deep Breathing (4-7-8)

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold gently for 7 seconds, exhale completely for 8 seconds. Repeat 3 to 4 times. Brings instant calm for anxiety and nervous tension.

🧠

2. Mindful 5-Minute Check-in

Close your eyes, notice what you feel without judging or resisting. Ask yourself: "How am I feeling right now?" Acknowledge every thought with kindness.

🌙

3. Sleep & Night Relaxation

Before sleeping, disconnect from screens, breathe deeply, and allow your body to release today's tension and worries. Perfect for quiet rest.

💛

4. Gratitude & Joy Moment

Take 2 minutes to reflect on 3 positive moments today. Transforming stress into appreciation elevates your dopamine and settles anxiety.

Developed by integrative medicine specialists, extending the exhale triggers the vagus nerve, rapidly lowering heart rate and switching your body from "fight-or-flight" to "rest-and-digest".
