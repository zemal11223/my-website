🧘 Meditate & Find Your Calm

Simple daily practices to bring peace to your mind

🌬️

1. Deep Breathing (4-7-8)

Inhale 4 sec, hold 7 sec, exhale 8 sec. Repeat 3 times. Instant calm for anxiety and stress. Do it every morning.

🧠

2. Mindful 5-Minute Check-in

Close eyes, notice what you feel without judging. Ask: "How am I feeling right now?" Write it in app daily.

🌙

3. Sleep Meditation

Before sleep, listen to soft music, breathe slowly, let go of today's worries. Helps with purple 😴 mood.

💛

4. Gratitude Moment

Daily 2 minutes: Think of 3 good things today. Turns sad 😢 or anxious 😰 into happy 😊. Best for evening.

🎧
